Use this in Chrome on your phone. Send prints that order's station slips; Print all prints the full board (every open order, pooled). Enter the Petpooja KOT number for every order. Tap Ready on each dish as it comes off the station; ready dishes drop off the slips. Each order is due 12 minutes after it's sent. Pick your printer in Chrome's print screen; a Bluetooth thermal printer needs its Android print service installed.

Kitchen board · all open orders

Each dish is listed by start-by time: the order's due time (sent + 12 min) minus 1 min to pack, minus the dish's cook time (fried rice 5, naan 3, roti 2, tikka 8, gravy 3…). Earliest start first; NOW means its start time has passed. The same dish from different KOTs is cooked together when their start times are close: wok within 6 min (up to 3), fryer within 6 min (up to 4), gravy + rice any time (hot box), tandoor within 2 min (up to 8, breads must be fresh). Ready dishes drop off.
