How it works: a scheduled Claude task opens Petpooja's Item Report for today in your Chrome every 10 minutes, reads the dish quantities, and sends them to the admin. The admin turns them into ingredient use with the same recipes as the nightly stock run, subtracts it from last night's closing stock (and adds today's purchases), and shows the result here. The sales are never written into stock until tonight's normal upload, and a count you enter always wins.
Needs: your PC on with Chrome signed in to Petpooja during service. If a read fails or is over 30 minutes old, this tab says so in red instead of showing old numbers as live.
Honest limits: the recipe grams are the same estimates as everywhere (butter, peas, spring onion and so on are still being tuned), and anything you sell without it being in the item report (staff meals, waste) isn't counted until the next count.
