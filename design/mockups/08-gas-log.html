Two cylinders, one momentWeighing both at the same time every night (12:30 AM, after the last orders) makes each day comparable. The old log had one cylinder at random hours, so a day's use was guesswork.
Staff only type the scale readingThe 20 kg empty weight is subtracted for them. If a cylinder is much heavier than last night, the app asks whether a new one was fitted, so a swap never shows up as "gas appeared".
What you get from itGas left and days left per cylinder (so a swap is never a surprise at lunch), real gas cost per dish, tandoor vs kitchen split, and a check of cylinder bills against what was actually burned.
