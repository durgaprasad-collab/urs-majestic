Quote to paid in one placeThe two catering orders so far were typed into the database by hand. Now an order is created here, moves Quote → Confirmed (advance) → In prep → Delivered → Paid, and the balance due is always visible.
Margin before you quoteDishes are picked from the menu, so the food cost comes from the same recipes as the menu. Change a catering rate and the margin updates, so a discount never quietly eats the profit. *Curd Rice isn't on the menu, so it has no cost yet.
Nothing runs short on the dayThe order's ingredients are added to Buy the day before the event, on top of normal service. Paneer, spring onion and soya sauce would be flagged today.
