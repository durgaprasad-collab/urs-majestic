Why one inboxRequisitions had 2 requests ever, but stock runs out every day. Now staff requests and "the stock says buy" sit in the same list, sorted by where you buy them, so one look tells you what to send for.
Approve = it's on the listApproving puts an item on the "waiting" tab. When anyone logs a purchase of that item, it moves to "bought" by itself, the same matching requisitions already use. Rejecting a staff request still notifies them.
Quantities are the owner's callStaff only send the item name from the app (no quantity, unit or note fields). The suggested quantity comes from what you usually buy and how fast it's used; edit it in place and the cost updates. Prices are your average paid price.
Fixed while building thisThe Order Forecast and the staff app's Stock tab were reading stock from an experimental model that had gone negative (e.g. Coriander −2,680 kg). This mockup uses the real stock ledger; the fix ships with this screen.
