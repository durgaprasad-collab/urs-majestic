1 · Royal (dark)The sidebar uses the logo's own deep green, so the full circular logo sits seamlessly at the top. Section headings are in Cinzel (the same carved serif as "MAJESTIC"), and the active page glows gold. The most on-brand option.
2 · Ivory (light)A cream sidebar with the logo as a small app-icon tile and the name in Cinzel. The active page is a deep-green pill. Calmer and lighter on the eyes for long sessions, so the numbers stand out more than the chrome.
3 · Top bar (no sidebar)The logo and name sit in a dark top bar, and pages live in two dropdowns ("Run the day" is shown open). The dashboard gets the full screen width, the most eagle-eye of the three, but pages take one extra click.
