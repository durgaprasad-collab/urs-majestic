A bill, not a lineUsha and Durga log 10–16 lines from one Hyperpure or Farmers Factory order. One form per bill with the supplier and bill number typed once saves most of the typing and keeps every line traceable to its bill.
Units can't driftThe unit is fixed to how the item is tracked (Coriander in bunches, Onion in kg). Typing grams for a kilo item converts automatically. That's what caused the 25 entries above and the 200 "kg" of Tomato Sauce.
Read the bill photoTake a photo of any bill, printed or handwritten. AI reads the supplier, date, bill number and every line, matches each to your items and units, and fills the form. Lines it isn't sure about are marked; nothing saves until you press Save.
Price check while typingEach line shows the per-unit price against what you usually pay. A price far off (a typo or a real jump) is flagged before saving; real jumps appear in Price watch.
