Reminder at 10 PM, every dayThe app schedules the alert on each staff phone, so it works without extra setup and even offline. Once tonight's count is saved, that night's reminder is skipped. It's in English, Bengali and Hindi.
Fewer items, less typingDaily: 54 perishable and running-low items (about 10 minutes). Monday: the full 126. Each number saves the moment it's entered, so there's no Save button to forget. "Same" fills the expected amount, and "Next" jumps to the next item.
Admins correct, staff countClick ✎ next to any quantity to correct it. It saves as a real count with your name and "admin edit", so the history shows who changed what. The admin-only ⋯ menu has Disable item for things no longer used, with a reason. Disabled items vanish from counts, forecasts, reorder and staff requests, and can be re-enabled from "Disabled items".
Admin at a glanceThree numbers (tonight's count, running out, needs a recount) and one list with problems first. Estimates are labelled "est." so a recipe estimate is never mistaken for a real count.
