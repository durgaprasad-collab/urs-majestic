How it works: items that recipes cover show daily use by sales (green tag), the average of the last 7 sales days, with the busiest day. Everything else shows by buying (blue tag): how much is bought ÷ days between bills, with "bought every N days". When the two disagree by more than 25%, a line under it says so: buying slower than sales often means bills weren't logged; buying faster means waste, staff use, or recipe grams set too low. Cover switches to the sales figure for recipe items, so Paneer shows 1.0 days instead of 1.5.
